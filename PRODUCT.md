# מדד השתתפות ביישובים 2026

## Product
Live election-day turnout dashboard for Israel’s 26th Knesset elections (27.10.2026). Tracks weighted turnout across 44 localities in five research groups versus historical finals (2020–2022).

## Audience
Journalists, analysts, and civic readers who need a clear live picture of participation by group — not seat forecasts or vote-choice inference.

## Purpose
Show what is known now: group turnout rates, coverage quality, progress vs 2022, day trend from real history points only. Gate numeric end-of-day forecasts until verified hourly history exists.

## Operating context
Hebrew RTL, dense “election-night control room” reading under time pressure. Trust signals: source, last update, partial coverage. No demo data on the production path.

## Constraints
Vanilla single-page HTML; keep existing data/JS contracts; no invented forecast numbers; accessibility and tabular numerals matter more than ornament.
